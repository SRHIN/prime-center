Advancing Health Equity Through Research, Innovation & Community Partnerships

We advance scientific excellence and evidence-driven innovation to improve reproductive health, strengthen infectious disease prevention and response, and champion maternal and child health equity across diverse communities. Our work also addresses nutrition and supports the prevention of vaccine-preventable cancers.

About PRIME

About the Center

Partnership for Reproductive Health, Infectious Disease & Maternal and Child Health Equity Center (PRIME) is a multidisciplinary hub where health leaders, scientists, clinicians, data experts, and community partners collaborate to address the most pressing challenges in reproductive health, infectious diseases, maternal and child health, nutrition, and cancer. We translate cutting-edge research into scalable solutions that strengthen health systems, drive equitable health outcomes, and inform global policy.

Our Foundation

Our Mission and Vision

verified

Our Mission

To advance evidence-driven research and innovative solutions that improve reproductive health, strengthen infectious disease prevention and response, and promote maternal and child health equity, while supporting better nutrition and cancer prevention.

visibility

Our Vision

A world where every individual, especially mothers and children, has equitable access to high-quality, culturally responsive healthcare, with better nutrition and stronger opportunities for disease prevention.

Focus Areas

Key Focus Areas

Where science, innovation, and community needs intersect.

Reproductive Health

Advancing high-impact research and strategic interventions that improve reproductive autonomy, health outcomes, and access to quality sexual and reproductive healthcare.

Infectious Diseases

Leading epidemiological research, surveillance innovations, and community-centered interventions to reduce the burden of infectious diseases and enhance outbreak preparedness.

Nutrition & Food Security

Supporting community-led approaches that address nutrition challenges, improve access to nutritious food, and strengthen health and wellbeing among underserved communities.

Vaccine-Preventable Cancer

Advancing awareness, education, and advocacy to support the prevention of vaccine-preventable cancers.

Maternal, Newborn and Child Health Innovation

We design and evaluate interventions that close equity gaps, improve service coverage, enhance care quality, and ensure safe, dignified, and culturally appropriate care for mothers and children.
