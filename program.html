Programs & Initiatives

Explore some of our impactful and life-changing programs

Program

The INCLUDE Project

The INCLUDE Project aims to improve access to HIV self-testing (HIVST) and prevention services for young people, especially those living with disabilities (PWDs), in Nigeria.

Learn More north_east
Program

MamaKid Project

Our innovative transformative solution leveraging a community-led, human-centerd design and gender transfornative approach to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality.

Learn More north_east
Program

The IMPACT Project

The IMPACT Project aims to reduce HIV infections and improve testing uptake through youth-led, community-based interventions. This would be achieved through empowering adolescents and young adults in IDP camps with HIV knowledge to lead community campaigns.

Learn More north_east
Program

Community Against Sexual Violence

Addressing sexual violence in Internally Displaced Persons(IDP) camps through skill acquisition programs.

Learn More north_east
Program

The HANDS Project

Vulnerable children lack access to proper hand hygiene and good hand-washing practices, which puts them at risk of contracting and spreading infectious diseases.

Learn More north_east
Program

Dettol Quest

The Dettol Quest Hygiene Programme Impact Assessment aimed at examining the impact of an innovative hygiene intervention on the knowledge, attitude, behavior, and practices toward proper sanitation and hygiene in schools, communities, and healthcare facilities in Lagos, Kwara and Abuja.

Learn More north_east
Program

Stop COVID-19

We have reached millions of vulnerable people with COVID-19 prevention information by translating and sharing evidence-based public health messages in over 100 languages!

Eradicate Hepatitis B
